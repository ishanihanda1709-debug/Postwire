Privacy notice

What we keep

  • Account: your name, email address, and a one-way scrambled form of your password (we cannot read your password). If you use "Continue with Google", Google tells us your name, email address and a Google account number, and we never see your Google password.
  • Your content: posts you save, and sample posts you paste into "My style".
  • Usage: how many AI drafts you used today, and your trial or subscription dates.
  • Session cookie: one cookie that keeps you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.

Sign-up notifications

When you create an account, your name, email address and sign-up date are also sent to a form service (Formspree) so the owner is told about the new sign-up and can contact you about your trial or subscription. Your password is never sent.

Visitor analytics

If switched on, we use Microsoft Clarity on our public pages and inside the app to see how people use the site (for example where they click and where they get stuck). Inside the app, the stories, posts and account details on screen are masked, so they are not recorded. Clarity is not used when your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal. Clarity's own privacy terms apply to the data it collects.

Payments

When you subscribe, you pay on a secure page run by Dodo Payments, our payment provider. We never see or store your card details. Dodo Payments shares with us your name, email address and the status of your subscription, so we can switch your access on and off.

AI processing

When you ask for drafts, the headline, the feed's short summary and your sample posts are sent to an AI service (currently Anthropic) to write the drafts, but only when the built-in AI is switched on. If you use the copy-the-prompt option instead, the text goes to whichever chatbot you paste it into, under that chatbot's own terms. Do not paste anything confidential into "My style".

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not read your LinkedIn account or post for you.

Your choices

You can delete your account and all your saved posts and sample posts from the Account page at any time. For other requests, contact ishanihanda1709@gmail.com.

Where it is stored and for how long

The app runs on Render (servers in Singapore). Accounts, saved posts and sample posts are stored in a database with Upstash (hosted in Mumbai, India). We keep your data for as long as your account exists, and delete it when you delete your account. News headlines we fetch are kept for 30 days. Your sign-up details also sit in our Formspree sign-up list, and you can ask us to remove them at any time.
