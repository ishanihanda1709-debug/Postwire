# Your voice

The app writes LinkedIn posts in your style. To make them sound like you, copy
`my-posts.example.md` to `my-posts.md` and paste 3 to 6 of your own past posts,
separated by a line containing only `---`. That file stays on your machine
(it is git-ignored). The app uses it to match your rhythm, vocabulary and tone,
never to copy your content.
