What it does

Everything between “saw it” and “posted it”.

Built for marketers who want a steady stream of real material, not generic content.

Auto-refreshing feed

Pulls RSS from marketing publications on a schedule, with publication dates and a link to every original. Duplicate coverage is merged into one card.

Campaigns and activations

Browse by campaign, case study or news, filter by topic, and switch between India and global. Images and videos are credited and linked, never re-hosted.

Case-study breakdowns

Idea, audience, strategy, execution and why it worked. The AI writes “Not stated in the source” rather than guess, and shows how much of the article it read.

LinkedIn drafts

Three angles per story, written to avoid AI clichés and to leave [brackets] where your own experience belongs. Edit, count characters, copy.

Your portfolio

Save the stories you break down, with your notes and chosen draft. Export the lot as Markdown.

Editorial, responsive UI

Bold serif typography, featured section, search, dark mode and a layout that works on a phone. No framework and no dependencies.

Built to be trusted

Facts and AI are never blurred.

If you post it under your name, you need to know what came from where.

Sourced

From the publisher

  • Headline, outlet, publication date
  • Feed summary, image and video, with credit
  • A link to the original, always
  • Nothing is bundled or invented
AI-generated

Analysis you verify

  • Breakdowns limited to what the article says
  • Evidence basis and confidence shown
  • Drafts use only sourced facts
  • Works without a key: you get a worksheet and draft scaffolds
How it works

Four steps, on repeat.

1

Fetch

Feeds are read in parallel and anything older than 60 days is dropped.

2

Dedupe

Canonical URLs and headline similarity merge the same story across outlets.

3

Enrich

Missing images, summaries and videos are read from the article's own metadata.

4

Create

Open a story, get the breakdown and drafts, edit, and save to your portfolio.

Quick start

Runs on Node. No dependencies.

Requires Node 20 or newer. The first refresh starts automatically.

git clone https://github.com/ishanihanda1709-debug/Linkedin-Automation.git
cd Linkedin-Automation
npm start

# optional: AI breakdowns and drafts
ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=your-key npm start

Then open http://127.0.0.1:3000. Source feeds are listed in sources.json and their health appears in the app's Sources tab.

Good to know

Honest answers.

Is this a hosted site?

No. It is a small Node app you run yourself, because it fetches feeds and stores your portfolio on disk. This page only describes it. To host the app publicly, use any Node host with a persistent disk and set ACCESS_TOKEN.

Does it show campaign launch dates?

It shows publication dates. RSS feeds do not provide launch dates, and the app does not guess them.

Do the feeds always work?

Publishers change feed addresses. The Sources tab shows each feed's status and error, and you can fix or disable any of them in sources.json.

Where do images and videos come from?

From the publisher's feed or article page. They are linked with credit and not copied. Videos load only when you click play.

Does it post to LinkedIn for me?

No. It drafts, you edit and copy. Posting stays with you.
