    What it does
    
Three things, nothing else.

    
Open it, pick a story, post.

    

      
See what's trending
Campaigns and marketing news from Indian and global publications, refreshed automatically. Stories covered by several outlets rise to the top. Every card shows its date and links to the original.

      
Get a post, not a paragraph
Click a story and get a short LinkedIn post: a hook, one idea per line, a clear lesson. It follows the rhythm of your own past posts. Edit it and copy.

      
Get the pictures too
Download a ready-made text card for your post, a photo card, or the publisher's original image with credit. All sized for LinkedIn.

    

  


  

    Built to be trusted
    
Posts that stick to the facts.

    
If you post it under your name, it has to be true.

    

      
What the post can say

        
  • Only what the source reports
  • No invented numbers, quotes or results
  • No made-up personal stories
  • A source line at the end

      
What you add

        
  • Your own opinion and experience
  • Your past posts, so it sounds like you
  • A final read before you post
  • Credit for any publisher image you use

    Quick start
    
Runs on Node. No dependencies.

    
Requires Node 20 or newer. The first refresh starts automatically.

    
git clone https://github.com/ishanihanda1709-debug/Linkedin-Automation.git
cd Linkedin-Automation
npm start

# optional: have the app write posts itself (paid AI key)
# ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=your-key npm start
# No key needed: copy a prompt into a free chatbot and paste the answer back

    
    
Then open http://127.0.0.1:3000. Paste a few of your own past posts into voice/my-posts.md so the writing sounds like you. Feeds are listed in sources.json.

    Good to know
    
Honest answers.

    
Is this a hosted site?
No. It is a small Node app you run yourself. This page only describes it. To host the app publicly, use any Node host and set ACCESS_TOKEN.

    
Does it read my LinkedIn feed?
No. LinkedIn does not allow automated access to profiles or feeds. Instead, you paste a few of your own posts into a file and the app matches their style.

    
Do I have to pay for an AI key?
No. Press one button to copy a ready-made prompt, paste it into a free chatbot such as Claude.ai, ChatGPT or Gemini, then paste the answer back. The app splits it into three editable options and makes matching picture cards. A paid key or a free local model only automates that step.

    
Can I use the publisher's images?
You can download them, but they belong to the publisher. Credit them or check usage rights. The text card is your own graphic and free to use.

    
Does it post to LinkedIn for me?
No. You edit, copy and post yourself.
