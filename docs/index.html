Built to be trusted

You stay in charge of what you post.

  • Headlines only: no publisher photos or article text, and every card links to the original.
  • Drafts use only facts from the headline and the feed's short summary. They never invent numbers, quotes or stories about you.
  • Finance drafts never give investment advice, price targets or predictions.
  • Postwire never reads or touches your LinkedIn. You review each draft and publish it yourself.
  • Your saved posts and style samples are private to your account, and you can delete your account any time.

FAQ

Good to know.

Does it post to LinkedIn for me?

No. You copy the draft, or open LinkedIn's post box with the text filled in, review it, and publish.

Can it make pictures for my posts?

It makes text graphics itself, in LinkedIn sizes. For a picture that matches the story, it writes an image prompt from the headline (no people, logos or text) that you paste into any AI image tool. Postwire does not use publishers' photos.

Does it sound like me?

Paste a few of your own posts under My style and every draft follows your rhythm and tone. It copies how you write, never what you wrote.

Do I need to pay for AI?

No. Postwire gives you a ready-made prompt that asks for three LinkedIn posts (story-led, lesson-led and question-led) in your own style. Copy it into any AI chatbot you like, including free and open-source ones, then paste the answer back. Postwire splits it into three options you can edit. A paid or local AI key only automates that step.

Where do the headlines come from?

Public news feeds from publications in each section. Each card names its publisher and links to the original. The newest stories come first, and you choose a 3, 7 or 30 day window.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. New accounts get 7 days free with no card needed. After that, a simple monthly plan keeps your drafts and saved posts.
